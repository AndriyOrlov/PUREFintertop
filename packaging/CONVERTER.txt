PureRef <-> FrameRef Converter
Updated 14 October 2026

1. Run PureRef-FrameRef-Converter.exe. Python is not needed.
   You can also drop a .pur or main.frameref onto the program icon.
2. Click "Choose file..." and pick a .pur or a FrameRef main.frameref.
   The direction is chosen automatically and a new output name is suggested;
   "Change..." picks another place.
3. Click "Convert". "Show result" opens the output in Explorer and
   "Open report" shows every simplification.

PureRef -> FrameRef
A new folder <name>-FrameRef appears. Open its main.frameref in FrameRef.
Move the whole folder: the images are in its content folder.

FrameRef -> PureRef
A new <name>-PureRef.pur appears with a .pur.conversion.json report next to it.

Originals are never changed and existing files are never overwritten:
a busy name gets " (2)", " (3)" and so on.

What is transferred
Images, positions, scale, rotation, mirroring, opacity, layer order, locks,
grayscale and text notes (including their font size).

Simplifications (listed in the report)
PureRef groups are flattened; items keep their places.
Crops and selected animation frames are saved as PNG.
PureRef pen drawings and FrameRef colour swatches become images.
FrameRef connections become static curves; live bindings are lost.
Text may wrap differently. PureRef comments and FrameRef tags, ratings,
borders and overlays are not transferred.

Not supported yet (conversion stops and explains why)
FrameRef video, PDF, portals and native drawings; local image zoom/pan or
crop offset; perspective transforms; PureRef images that are only linked
(embed them first: pur-adapter embed); non-rectangular or fractional crops.

Checks
112 automated tests. Boards converted from FrameRef were opened, rendered and
resaved by PureRef 2.1.3 without warnings. Opening converted boards in
FrameRef itself is still to be checked.
